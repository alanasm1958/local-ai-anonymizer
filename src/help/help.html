How to use Local AI Anonymizer

Hides private details before they reach AI tools. Takes 1 minute to read.

What it does

When you paste text into an AI chat, names, emails, phone numbers, ID and card numbers are swapped for labels like [PERSON_1]. The AI never sees the real details. When it answers, you see the real names again, on your screen only.

How to use it

  1. Work as usual. Paste, drag or type into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or another supported AI site. A small panel shows what was hidden.
  2. Read the answer. Real names appear in the reply on your screen. The AI site still only has the labels.
  3. Need the real details back? Select the AI's reply and press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on Mac). The copy has the real details, ready to paste into your email or document. The site's own Copy button gives labels only. For a file the AI made: extension icon, then Restore a file.
  4. Files: click the extension icon, then Clean a file. You get a cleaned copy of your Word, Excel, PowerPoint or text file to upload instead.

Can't see the icon? Click the puzzle piece next to the address bar and pin Local AI Anonymizer.

Is it safe?

  • Everything happens on your computer. No data is sent to the developer or any other service.
  • The real details are kept in memory only and are erased when you close the browser. Restore what you need before closing it.
  • No account or sign-in is needed.

Please remember

  • It catches most private details, not all. Glance at the text before you send.
  • Names written in Arabic letters are not hidden yet. Remove them yourself.
  • It only cleans what goes in. Don't use AI tools for data your policy says must not leave Amideast systems at all.
